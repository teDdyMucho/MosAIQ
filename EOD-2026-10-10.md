## Bugs found and fixed

1. **Reduced-motion hid the video entirely.** I had written
   `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce { display:none }`. Your Windows has
   animations off (`MinAnimate = 0`), so the hero rendered blank — and would
   have for every user with that setting. A slow landscape shot is not what
   that preference is meant to suppress. Removed; the note on how to re-add it
   properly is in the CSS.

2. **The email field stretched to ~500px tall** on mobile once the form became
   a column — `align-items: stretch` with no fixed height. Pinned to 54px.

3. **Mobile gap used `clamp(48px, 11vw, 72px)`** — at 430px wide that resolves
   to 47px, below the floor I intended. Replaced with a flat 76px.

   will not work.
