A teacher is one link in a long chain

Pirkei Avot opens by tracing how the Torah has been handed from teacher to student since Sinai. Every lecture, and every question you ask, continues that chain.

  1. סיניSinai. The Torah is given to the Jewish people.
  2. משהMoshe. Moses receives the Torah and teaches it to the nation.
  3. יהושעYehoshua. Joshua receives it from Moses.
  4. זקניםThe Elders. They carry it through the generations after Joshua.
  5. נביאיםThe Prophets. They guard it and call the people back to it.
  6. כנסת הגדולהThe Great Assembly. Its sages pass it on with the charge to raise many students.
Source: Pirkei Avot 1:1

FAQ

Questions before you begin.

Is RavMizAI actually Rabbi Mizrachi?

No. RavMizAI is an AI conversation experience built around selected public teachings, lectures and materials associated with Rabbi Yosef Mizrachi. The answers are generated by AI, not spoken live by Rabbi Mizrachi. The purpose is to make the teaching archive easier to explore conversationally, but users should still return to the original lectures and Torah sources when accuracy or context matters.

Can I ask halachic questions?

You can ask RavMizAI to explain general Torah concepts, public teachings and background around halachic topics. But it should not be treated as a posek or as a replacement for a qualified rabbi. Personal questions about Shabbat, kashrut, family matters, medical situations, business issues or any case that depends on your exact circumstances should be brought to a competent rabbi who can ask follow-up questions and give a real ruling.

What can I ask about?

RavMizAI is intended for the kinds of subjects Rabbi Mizrachi addresses publicly: teshuvah, emunah, Shabbat, Jewish identity, purpose, free will and Divine Providence, mussar, Torah and science, parashah themes, and practical spiritual growth. You can begin in ordinary language, ask follow-up questions, request a clearer explanation, or ask where a particular idea comes from in the broader lecture archive.

Is there a free option?

The public website is free to browse, but live RavMizAI voice and text conversations are available only after signing in. New users can start with the 7-day trial flow, which creates an account, verifies the email address and then unlocks the member conversation experience, transcripts, preferences, wallet and billing controls.
