Data Use & Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Launch note: This is a product-ready draft structure, not legal advice. Confirm actual vendors, retention periods, state/privacy-law disclosures and the operating entity before launch.

1. Scope

This policy explains how RavMizAI handles information when you use its websites, accounts, AI conversations, voice features, trial, subscription, wallet, billing and personalization features.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the feature, we may collect name, email, authentication data, subscription status, payment-status tokens from a payment processor, preferences, support communications, device/browser data and usage analytics.

3. AI Conversations

When you use text or voice features, your prompts, transcriptions and AI responses may be processed to provide the conversation, preserve continuity, enforce safety controls and improve service reliability. Production settings should specify whether full transcripts are stored and how long they are retained.

4. Voice Data

Voice audio may be transmitted to speech-processing or AI service providers to transcribe and respond to your request. RavMizAI should not create biometric voiceprints for identity recognition unless a separate notice and legally required consent are implemented.

5. How We Use Information

  • Provide and secure accounts and AI features.
  • Process subscriptions and trial status.
  • Remember saved preferences.
  • Send verification, billing, security and support messages.
  • Analyze service reliability and improve safety and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent abuse.

6. AI and Service Providers

RavMizAI may use third-party providers for AI generation, speech processing, hosting, authentication, email, analytics and payments. We should share only information reasonably necessary for each provider to perform its service and contractually restrict independent advertising uses where appropriate.

7. Payments

Full card details should be collected and stored by a PCI-compliant payment processor rather than RavMizAI servers. RavMizAI may receive payment-method metadata, transaction status, billing identifiers and subscription information.

8. Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies for session functionality, preferences, security, analytics and, if enabled with appropriate consent, marketing measurement.

9. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell private Torah reflections or AI conversation content. Information may be disclosed to service providers, professional advisers or authorities when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

10. Data Retention

Different categories of data may be retained for different periods depending on account status, security needs, legal obligations and product settings. The production policy should publish the actual retention schedule for voice audio, transcripts, account records and billing records.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, and to opt out of certain marketing or data-sharing activities. Requests may be sent to privacy@ravmizai.com.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No internet system can be guaranteed completely secure.

13. International Users

If you access RavMizAI from outside the country where the operator or its providers are located, information may be processed in other jurisdictions subject to appropriate legal safeguards where required.

14. Children

The current service design is intended for adults age 18 and older unless a separate age-appropriate experience, consent flow and policy are implemented.

15. Changes

We may update this policy as the Services change. Material changes should be communicated as required by law.

16. Contact

Privacy: privacy@ravmizai.com
Support: support@ravmizai.com
